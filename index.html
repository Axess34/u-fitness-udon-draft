The Gym · ยิม

Luxury training. Affordable prices.

U Fitness sits in the heart of Udon Thani by Preecha Market (ตลาดปรีชา). Inside it's cool, clean and spacious, with racks, plate-loaded and pin-loaded machines, a full dumbbell wall and a cardio line-up.

Come for a single session, a week or the whole month; there's a pass to suit how you train.

  • Fully air-conditioned, open 06:00 – 23:00 every day
  • Daily, weekly & monthly passes
  • Group classes included for members
  • Personal trainers for 1:1 coaching
